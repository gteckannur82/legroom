    RRoof · Scanning

    Start with the whole building.

    Scan your whole drive, your home folder, or any folder you drop on the window. A normal scan needs no special permission. Fast scan reads the drive’s own index directly and sees every folder, even the ones Windows keeps to itself.

    • 4,966,331 files and folders in 21.5 seconds: a Fast scan of a full 465 GB drive, measured with Legroom’s benchmark tool on a development PC with an NVMe SSD.
    • Stop whenever you like. A stopped scan keeps what it found and says the totals are partial.
    • Nothing is uploaded. Every scan runs on your PC.

    6Overview

    Every gigabyte has an address.

    The Overview adds up to your drive’s exact capacity: what you scanned, what’s elsewhere, what Windows keeps for itself, the Recycle Bin and the free space. Anything that can’t be accounted for is labelled, not hidden.

    • Real size on disk. A 64 GB virtual disk that only uses 12 GB counts as 12 GB, which is what deleting it would actually free.
    • Each suggestion names its cost: rebuilds itself, rebuildable, or re-downloadable.
    • Your own files are never suggested.

    5Storage Map

    See the whole floor plan.

    Every folder becomes a rectangle sized by the space it really takes. Click into any folder, then colour the map by file type, by age, or by what’s safe to clear.

    • Thousands of tiny files become one labelled tile instead of unreadable crumbs.
    • Colour by age to spot what nobody has opened in years.
    • Right-click anything to open it, show it in Explorer, or add it to cleanup.

    4Search & filters

    Find anything while you type.

    Search millions of files by name, size, type and age, instantly. Fifteen ready-made filters find the usual suspects (old downloads, videos, installers, caches, game libraries, AI models) and show how much each one holds.

    • Plain words just work: type invoice.
    • Or be precise: kind:video >1GB, older:1y in:Downloads.
    • Totals never count twice. A folder and the files inside it add up once.

    3Safe cleanup

    Nothing goes without a review.

    Tick what you want gone, anywhere in Legroom. Before anything moves, a review re-measures every item, shows where it will go, and lists anything Legroom refuses to touch with the reason.

    • Two stages. Items go to the Recycle Bin first and can all be put back. Space is freed only when you remove them permanently.
    • Only inside your own folder. Windows, Program Files and other accounts are never touched.
    • Protected even there: SSH keys, saved passwords, certificates, mail, phone backups and OneDrive.

    2Applications

    Know what every app really costs.

    Every installed app, desktop programs and Store apps alike, with the space its folder actually takes, measured rather than taken from the installer. Uninstall right there, and find the folders that apps you already removed left behind.

    • Measured, not claimed. An installer’s own estimate appears only as a fallback, marked ≈.
    • Uninstall the proper way: each app’s own uninstaller runs, so nothing is left half-removed.
    • Leftovers go through the same review as everything else.

    1System Monitor

    And what the building is doing right now.

    Processor, memory, disks, network and graphics, live, with every running program. The figures come from the same counters Task Manager uses, so they match.

    • End a stuck program. Legroom asks first, and never offers to end the parts of Windows that would crash it.
    • Sort by processor, memory or disk to find what’s slowing you down.
    • Paused when you leave the view, so it costs nothing in the background.
